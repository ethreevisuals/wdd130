Featured Trip Packages

Half-Day Express

Difficulty: Class II - III (Beginner Friendly)

Perfect for families and first-time rafters! Enjoy scenic river stretches mixed with exciting splash rapids. Snacks and safety gear included.

Full-Day White Water Challenge

Duration: 6-7 Hours

Difficulty: Class III - IV (Intermediate)

Our most popular trip! Navigate continuous technical rapids, enjoy a riverside deli lunch, and explore hidden canyon waterfalls along the route.

Multi-Day Wilderness Expedition

Duration: 3 Days / 2 Nights

Difficulty: Class IV - V (Advanced)

The ultimate backcountry experience. Conquer wild rapids by day and relax under starry skies by night with gourmet campfire meals prepared by our guides.
