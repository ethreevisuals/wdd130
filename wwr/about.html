Whitewater Rafting Company

We live for the roar of the river. Our purpose is to connect thrill-seekers, families, and nature lovers with the raw power and beauty of white water through safe, expert-guided river expeditions.

History

What started in 2010 as two river guides and a single raft has grown into one of the region's most trusted outdoor adventure outfits.

Built on a deep respect for the water and a commitment to leave no trace, our team has safely navigated over 50,000 river miles while advocating for the protection of native river ecosystems.
